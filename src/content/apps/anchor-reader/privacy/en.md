# Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: July 27, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
Anchor ("the App"), developed by Alexandre Dupré, provides a focused reading experience for digital documents. We are committed to protecting your privacy through an offline-first architecture. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the handling of your information.

## 2. Offline-First Architecture
Anchor is designed to function entirely offline. All processing of your documents (including but not limited to EPUB, PDF, and TXT files) is performed locally on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to our servers or any third-party cloud services.

## 3. Information Collection & Sharing
While your documents and reading progress remain strictly local, we do collect anonymized usage data and crash reports to help us improve the App. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or exact location data. We do not sell or trade any information with third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Services
To monitor app stability and understand how features are used, the App integrates selected third-party services for anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting. These services may collect basic device information, OS version, and app interaction events, all of which are anonymized and cannot be traced back to your identity or reading material.

## 5. Data Security & Retention
The security of your data depends on the security of your physical device. Data is retained on your device until you manually delete it or uninstall the App. We recommend using standard device security features (passcode, biometric lock) to protect your local data.

## 6. User Rights
Under various privacy regulations (such as GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, or delete your data. In Anchor, you can exercise these rights at any time by modifying your settings or deleting the App and its associated local data.
